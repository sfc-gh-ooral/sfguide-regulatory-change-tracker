# Synthetic data

Everything here is generated by [`scripts/generate_data.py`](../scripts/generate_data.py) with a fixed seed, so every run produces the same files. The bank (Brivanta Bank), the regulators, the rules, the policies, the controls and the people are fictional and were written for this example. No real regulatory text is copied.

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `notices/` | 24 notices as PDFs, loaded by setup |
| `incoming/` | 1 notice held back, to watch the task pick up a new file |
| `csv/policies.csv` | 40 policies with owner team and owner |
| `csv/controls.csv` | 58 controls linked to policies, with owner and frequency |
| `csv/owners.csv` | Owner for each team |
| `csv/notice_files.csv` | Intake register: file, received time and feed |
| `csv/prior_gaps.csv` | 5 gaps from earlier reviews, so the tracker shows work in flight |

Regulators (all fictional): Retail Conduct Authority, Prudential Banking Board, Financial Data Protection Office, Payments Oversight Council, Anti-Money Laundering Directorate, Insurance Conduct Office, Securities Markets Commission.

## Planted stories

| Story | Where | What the pipeline finds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shorter complaint deadline | RCN-2026-004, requirement 3.2; POL-CMP-01 | The rule requires a final response within 15 business days; the procedure still allows 30 calendar days. Gap: Requirement not met. |
| Obligation with no control | RCN-2026-011, requirements 3.1 and 3.2 | Inventory and annual review of third-party AI models. The model risk policy covers only internally built models and no control matches (similarity under 0.55). Gaps: No control. |
| Conflicting policies | RCN-2026-007, requirement 3.1; POL-CMP-01 and POL-REC-01 | Complaint records must be kept 7 years. The complaint procedure keeps them 7 years, but the records retention standard deletes complaint correspondence after 5 years. Gap: Policy conflict. |
| Not applicable | RCN-2026-016 (and RCN-2026-017) | Annuity suitability for insurers, and best execution for broker-dealers. The bank sells neither, so AI_COMPLETE auto-dismisses them with a reason. |

The other notices are written so that the bank's policies and controls already meet them, to show what a clean mapping looks like. RCN-2026-023 is a consultation, and RCN-2026-025 is the held-back notice.
